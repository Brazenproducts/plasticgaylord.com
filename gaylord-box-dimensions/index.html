Complete Guide to PP Gaylord Boxes

This page covers the full specification, application detail, and sourcing guidance for PP Gaylord Boxes from Elipacko — the leading factory-direct manufacturer of PP corrugated packaging for food processing, agriculture, industrial, and cold-chain applications.

What Is PP Corrugated?

Polypropylene corrugated sheet is manufactured by extruding PP resin through a corrugated die, creating a rigid twin-wall panel with a hollow fluted core. The result is a lightweight, waterproof, structurally rigid material that outperforms cardboard in virtually every performance category relevant to commercial packaging: moisture resistance, reusability, structural integrity under load, chemical resistance, and food-grade compliance.

PP Gaylord Boxes manufactured by Elipacko uses documented-grade PP resin compliant with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for direct food contact applications. This makes it suitable for USDA-inspected meat and poultry facilities, FDA-regulated produce operations, and food service distribution without requiring any additional protective liner or barrier.

Technical Specifications

Standard specifications for PP Gaylord Boxes:

  • Available wall thicknesses: 2mm, 4mm, 6mm, 8mm, 10mm
  • Flute profiles: B-flute, C-flute, X-flute — chosen based on load requirements
  • Operating temperature: -4°F to 212°F (-20°C to 100°C) standard; blast-freeze grades available
  • Colors: White, black, blue, red, yellow, green standard; custom colors for HACCP programs
  • Surface options: Smooth, textured, printed
  • FDA status: PP resin compliant with 21 CFR 177.1520

Performance Advantages Over Cardboard

The case for switching from cardboard to PP Gaylord Boxes is grounded in measurable operational improvements:

  • Waterproof: No absorption of moisture, blood, juices, or ice meltwater — critical in cold-chain and wet processing environments
  • 50+ reuse cycles: Each PP Gaylord Boxes replaces 50 cardboard boxes — a direct reduction in per-unit packaging cost over the product's service life
  • Sanitation-compatible: Pressure washable and sanitizable with food-grade cleaners; no degradation from repeated cleaning cycles
  • Structurally consistent: Does not lose rigidity when wet or in high-humidity environments — load capacity is maintained throughout the operating cycle
  • Recyclable at end of life: #5 polypropylene recycling stream available; no landfill-only disposal like waxed cardboard

Applications

PP Gaylord Boxes is used across a broad range of industries where packaging performance, sanitation compliance, and total cost of ownership matter. Primary applications include:

  • Fresh meat, poultry, and seafood processing and transport
  • Fresh produce harvest bins, field crates, and cold-chain distribution containers
  • Industrial returnable packaging for automotive, electronics, and general manufacturing
  • Cold storage and freezer logistics for food service and distribution
  • Agricultural bulk bins for grain, seed, and specialty crop handling
  • Food service reusable totes for restaurant and institutional supply chains
